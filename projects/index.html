Overview

Beyond The Horizon is a community for the events and fans of YouTuber The Horizon.

I joined the moderation team in January 2023 and staffed the 10vs1000 event. After that I moderated the community and worked behind the scenes.

In January 2026 I brought in Minekeep as a sponsor for 15vs1500, and got promoted to Production Manager.

After my promotion I took over management of 15vs1500. The first recording session had failed, so I rebuilt the event from scratch. I led the development, builds and gameplay to re-make the event within 4 months.

Since then I've worked with Horizon to release 2 more videos: the sponsored Wynncraft video, and an old unreleased video called Trolling Hypixel Skyblock.

Currently we are working on editing the 15vs1500 video and creating a team of voice actors to dub Horizon's content into different languages.

An upcoming project is CakeWars. Our test run (season 0) concluded in August 2026. You can view a clip of the finale on Twitch.

State is a community that produces large-scale Minecraft events for the YouTuber Ish.

I was part of the event staff team from November 2023 until July of 2025 and worked on the 1000 Players Simulate Civilization: Rich & Poor video.

I helped design a system to organise important lore events for the video and assisted event participants in tickets. The events were hosted at 2 AM in my timezone, so my primary role was to provide support. It was incredibly fun and insightful being part of such a large staff & production team.

Feel free to reach out to @mericy (manager of the event staff team) on Discord for a vouch.

Overview

HorizonMines is a Minecraft server I started in December of 2022, and after 9 months of development we released season 1.

Over the years HorizonMines has provided me with an unique environment where I can learn or improve skills without there being a lot of pressure. This has given me the ability to get experience with every aspect of having a server, everything from game design to marketing has been part of my role.

Out of all aspects, recruiting staff and conducting interviews is definitely the skill that I've been able to hone on this project. Practical experience beats any other preparation, and I'm happy that I've been able to master it.

I've had lots of fun creating HorizonMines, met amazing people because of it, and it is my most cherished project due ot the nostalgia from SkyLodge, one of the first servers I staffed on. I've always treated the servers as a passion project to continue the spirit.

If you'd like to get an idea of what it was like you can find a memorial video from one of the owners on YouTube.

Shoutout to @bl4nkie for making the amazing artwork above!

Overview

6b6t is the largest Minecraft anarchy server.

I was invited by the owner qbasty to join the team in June 2023 and was an adminsitrator until Decemeber 2023.

My responsibilities included setting up a moderation team, writing blogs and hosting community events.

This was one of my first positions managing a larger Discord server, and the fact that it was based around an anarchy server came with its challenges.

I learned how to deal with of threats and instigation, directed at myself or other staff members, on a level that I hadn't encountered before.
