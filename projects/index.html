State is a server that produces large-scale Minecraft events for the YouTuber Ish.

I helped design a system to organise important lore events for the video and assisted event participants in tickets. The events were hosted at 2 AM in my timezone, so my primary role was to provide support. It was incredibly fun being part of a larger staff team and the production process of such a big video.

I was part of the staff team from November 2023 until July of 2025. I  got to work on one video — 1000 Players Simulate Civilization: Rich & Poor.

Feel free to reach out to @mericy (manager of the event staff team) on Discord for a vouch.

Overview

HorizonMines is a skymining server which I started working on in December of 2022 together with @Caspie. We worked on the server for roughly 9 months before releasing Season I.

The server then had 4 more seasons until December 2025, when it went on hiatus between Season V and Season VI.

The server is currently in the development phase of Season VI, which has slowed down a bit by the BeyondTheHorizon event. The server is currently set to release somewhere in the summer!

Developing, growing and managing a server is quite the task, but over the years I've learned an unimaginable amount of things and met incredible people. HorizonMines is one of my most cherished projects as it brings back loads of memories from SkyLodge.

Overview

6b6t is the second-largest Minecraft anarchy server, but with the twist of having commands like /home & /tpa.

I was invited by the owner qbasty to join 6b6t as an administrator in June of 2023 to help manage the Discord. This was the first time I managed a larger Discord server, and it was quite challenging due to the Minecraft side being anarchy. I learned how to deal with things like threats, instigation and trolling directed at myself and other community members on a level I hadn't encountered before.

My responsibilities consisted of managing the staff team, hosting events and assisting in content creation.
